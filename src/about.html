Zam Ortega is a graphic designer and illustrator with a BA in Design.

Zam is a first-generation Mexican-American designer and illustrator living and working in the PNW. Her work blends bold storytelling with a keen eye for illustrative detail. As director of her graduating class’s design exhibition Layer By Layer, she led a team in curating a showcase of diverse creative voices.

On campus, she served as lead designer for The Spectator newspaper and created student-centered visual campaigns for the SU Health and Wellness Center. Zam is now open to full-time, contract, or freelance opportunities in design and illustration.
