Zam Ortega is a designer and illustrator with a BA in Design from``` Seattle University!

Zam's work blends bold storytelling with a keen eye for illustrative detail. As director of her graduating class’s design exhibition Layer By Layer, she led a team in curating a showcase of diverse creative voices.

On campus, she served as lead designer for The Spectator newspaper and created student-centered visual campaigns for the SU Health and Wellness Center. Zam is now open to full-time, contract, or freelance opportunities in design and illustration.
