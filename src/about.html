Zam Ortega is a graphic designer and illustrator with a BA in Design.

Zam is a first-generation Mexican-American designer and illustrator living and working in Eastern Washington. Her work blends bold storytelling with a keen eye for illustrative detail. She is inspired by the little things that come across her device and window screens.

Zam Likes: Salt and Straw Honey Lavender ice cream, Spicy Thai fried rice with mango, Muji A5 weekly planner, Mama Chia raspberry chunky drink.

Zam is now open to contract, or freelance opportunities in design and illustration.
